# Layers

Imagine a stack of transparent films over a sheet of paper. You can draw on each one separately and see the whole picture from above. Those are layers.

## Why you need them

- **Safety.** A sun on its own layer can be erased, moved or recoloured without touching the landscape.
- **Order.** Sketch, tone, shadows, details — each stage on its own layer.
- **Experiments.** Copy a layer and try a variant while keeping the original.

## The «Layers» panel

Layers are listed from top to bottom in the order they lie: the top one covers the ones below.

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| New layer | the «+» button at the bottom of the panel or **Ctrl+Shift+N** |
| Rename | double-click the name |
| Hide or show | the eye icon on the left |
| Change the order | drag the layer up or down |
| Delete | the bin button or the Delete key |

## The active layer

The brush always paints on the **active** layer — it is highlighted in the panel. The most common beginner's mistake is painting on the wrong layer. If a stroke "does not appear" or lands in the wrong place, first check which layer is selected.

## The background

An opened picture lies on the bottom «Background» layer. Empty parts of new layers are transparent: the background shows through them. The editor shows transparency as a grey-and-white checkerboard.

## Sensei's advice

Name layers right away. After an hour of work, «Layer 17» and «Layer 18» say nothing, while «Face shadows» and «Highlights» do.

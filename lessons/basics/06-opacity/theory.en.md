# Layer opacity and visibility

Every layer has two settings that do not change what is drawn, but change how it is seen.

## Opacity

Layer opacity (from 0% to 100%) sets how much the layer covers the layers below:

- **100%** — the layer is fully visible;
- **50%** — what lies below shows halfway through the layer;
- **0%** — the layer is not visible at all, but everything drawn on it is intact.

The “Opacity” field is at the top of the “Layers” panel.

### Layer or brush?

The brush has an opacity too, but it is "baked" into the stroke: you cannot change it later. Layer opacity acts on the whole layer at once, and you can change it as often as you like.

Hence the technique: paint a shadow on a separate layer at full strength, then adjust the layer opacity until the shadow sits right.

## Visibility

The **eye** icon to the left of a layer hides it completely. This is handy to:

- compare "before" and "after";
- hide the sketch to judge the clean drawing;
- keep a spare variant of a detail without showing it.

A hidden layer is left out when the picture is exported.

## Glazing

In painting, a glaze is a thin transparent layer of paint over a dried one. In the editor you make it like this: a new layer, a colour on top, opacity 20–40%. The drawing below shows through while the colour and tone change.

## Sensei's advice

Ink on washi is never "just black" — the paper breathes through it. Translucency gives a digital drawing the same breath.
